Before: ## Worked examples from everyday life

After: ## Working-rate (工作率) and fractions

Treat the whole job as 1. Use the basic relation:

Work = rate × time

So:
- rate = work / time
- time = work / rate

If A completes a job in 5 days, A's daily rate = 1/5 of the job. If B completes it in 10 days, B's rate = 1/10. Together their rate = 1/5 + 1/10 = 3/10 of the job per day. Time together = 1 ÷ (3/10) = 10/3 days ≈ 3.33 days.

When one leaves early, compute each person's work done (rate × days), subtract from 1 for remaining work, then use the remaining worker's rate to find extra time.

Convert rates to percentages by multiplying by 100 (e.g. 1/5 = 20% of the job per day).
